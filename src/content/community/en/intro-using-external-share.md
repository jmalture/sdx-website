## What External Share is good for

- **Offers and sales documents** on one page in your look, instead of three attachments in one e-mail.
- **Documents for investors or auditors**, with a password and a clear expiry date.
- **Collecting files:** turn on "Allow upload" and your partner uploads contracts, photos or reports straight into your folder.
- **Large data packages** such as test reports or plans that are too big for e-mail.
- **Trade fairs and events:** the QR code of the share on a stand takes visitors straight to your documents.

## Tips from practice

- **As few rights as necessary.** Someone who only reads needs neither upload nor delete.
- **Match the expiry date to the occasion.** An offer valid for 30 days? Let the share expire after 30 days.
- **Update the folder rather than sharing again.** Shared folders stay in sync, so your recipient sees the new version right away, under the same link.
- **Everything stays traceable.** Access to shares (create, view, download, upload) is recorded in the audit log.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can someone guess the link?** No. Share links are generated randomly and cannot be predicted.

**Does my recipient need an account?** No. A browser is enough, plus the password if you set one.

**Can I end a share early?** Yes. Under "Shared" in your profile you see all your shares and can manage them there.
